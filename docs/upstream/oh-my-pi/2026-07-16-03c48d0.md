## Review

The behavioral change makes a finite, non-negative OpenRouter-reported account charge authoritative over catalog-derived cost estimates. When estimated component costs exist, OMP scales them proportionally to the reported total; when they do not, it assigns the reported charge to input cost and zeros the other components. In both paths, the final total equals the provider-reported charge. [`openai-shared.ts`](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi/blob/03c48d073bd4849726cc14750b5aecfa310bdf26/packages/ai/src/providers/openai-shared.ts#L341-L362)

OMP applies the reconciliation after local cost calculation in both OpenAI Chat Completions and OpenAI Responses projections. [`openai-completions.ts`](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi/blob/03c48d073bd4849726cc14750b5aecfa310bdf26/packages/ai/src/providers/openai-completions.ts#L1629-L1634) · [`openai-shared.ts`](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi/blob/03c48d073bd4849726cc14750b5aecfa310bdf26/packages/ai/src/providers/openai-shared.ts#L2563-L2571)

Focused tests require both the final total and the sum of cost components to match the reported charge. [`usage-attribution.test.ts`](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi/blob/03c48d073bd4849726cc14750b5aecfa310bdf26/packages/ai/test/usage-attribution.test.ts#L70-L83) · [`openai-responses-openrouter.test.ts`](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi/blob/03c48d073bd4849726cc14750b5aecfa310bdf26/packages/ai/test/openai-responses-openrouter.test.ts#L301-L337)
